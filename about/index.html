About Us

Whitby's Local HVAC Team, Built on Durham Region Trust

From the heritage streets near Downtown Whitby to the fast-growing subdivisions of Brooklin, we keep homes along the north shore of Lake Ontario comfortable in every season.

Our Story

Rooted in Whitby, Serving All of Durham Region

Whitby HVAC Pros started with a simple promise: bring honest, skilled comfort care to the homes and small businesses of Durham Region, one neighbour at a time.

We live and work where you do, along the Dundas Street corridor, down toward the lakefront, and up into the newer builds of North Whitby and Brooklin. That local grounding shows up on every call, whether we're tracking down a clanking furnace in a century home near Downtown or sizing a ductless mini-split for a tight new-construction build. We understand this housing stock, and we know exactly what humid lakefront summers and deep Ontario winters throw at it.

Every job is handled by licensed technicians who treat your home the way they'd treat their own. That means tidy, code-compliant work, clear pricing before a wrench turns, and straight talk about whether a repair will carry you through the season or a replacement is the smarter long-term call. No upsells. No surprise fees on the invoice. Just the kind of service you'd happily recommend to the family next door.

As a family-owned company, our reputation has been built one household at a time across Whitby, Ajax, Pickering, and Oshawa. So when a no-heat emergency hits in the middle of a January cold snap, you reach a real local voice that knows the area and dispatches help fast.

Our Commitment

Our Commitment to Whitby Homeowners

Comfort shouldn't be a guessing game. From the first phone call to the final walkthrough, you get upfront quotes, technicians who show up on time, and workmanship that clears Ontario code and our own higher bar. Whether we're tuning an aging furnace, cleaning out ductwork, or installing a smart thermostat, the goal is the same: dependable comfort and lower energy bills, not a band-aid fix.

Behind every visit is a team that stands behind its work. We service every major brand of furnace, air conditioner, and heat pump, and we stay current on high-efficiency and ductless technology so your system performs through sticky lakefront summers and sub-zero February nights alike.
